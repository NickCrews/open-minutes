# Recurring mishearings and name sources

Speech recognition mangles the same local names the same way in every
meeting. Fix them where context makes the intended word clear. People's names
take the spelling in `packages/fixtures/test-data/people.jsonl`.

## Where to check spellings and who spoke

- **GBOS minutes** on muni.org, named like `GBOS May 18 2026 Minutes final.pdf`
  (search "GBOS <Month> <D> <YYYY> minutes"). They list the roster, mover and
  seconder of every motion as initials ("BB/KO"), who voted no, who gave each
  report, and public commenters by name. WebFetch saves the PDF without
  reading it; run `pdftotext` on the saved file.
- **Planning and Zoning Commission roster:** onboard.muni.org/board/2897.
- **Assembly members and youth representatives:** muni.org Assembly member
  profiles and muni.org/Youth.
- **GBOS candidates:** turnagainnews.org. **Legislators:** akleg.gov.
